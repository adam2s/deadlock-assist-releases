Changelog

What’s new in every version.

Highlight: since version 1.2.6 the advisor gets 81% of counters right (was 62%).

Version 1.2.9 Latest November 5, 2026

  • “Website” button in the app opens deadlock-assist.com.

Version 1.2.8 November 5, 2026

  • Full English app and overlay (PL / EN switch). Starts in your Windows language.

Version 1.2.7 November 5, 2026

  • History: each suggestion shows where it came from (“Your build” / “Vs enemies”) and why.

Version 1.2.6 November 5, 2026

  • The advisor compares items at equal farm: 81% good counters instead of 62% (test on 2,020 matches).
  • 29% of players’ real buys are in the advisor’s top 5 (was 25%).
  • The “Buy now” card has two rows: “Your build” and “Vs enemies”. Until 10:00 it counters your lane.
  • The card shows an enemy far ahead in souls and an item against them.
  • Three advisor modes: “My build”, “Balanced”, “Counters”.
Older versions (1.0.0–1.2.5)

Version 1.2.5 November 4, 2026

  • “Suggest a change” button (GitHub idea form) and “☕ Support” in the app window.

Version 1.2.4 November 4, 2026

  • “Overlay” tab: screen preview in the middle; clicking a panel scrolls to its settings.
  • Overlay clock: “What now” with a direction arrow, camps with a respawn bar.

Version 1.2.3 November 4, 2026

  • The farm chart no longer drops real readings when souls grow fast.

Version 1.2.2 November 4, 2026

  • History: minute-by-minute farm chart for every player and both teams.
  • History: farm gain every 5 minutes and a match summary with what to improve.

Version 1.2.1 November 4, 2026

  • Faster in the background.

Version 1.2.0 November 4, 2026

  • New “Updates” tab with the list of changes in every version.

Version 1.1.9 November 4, 2026

  • Fixed an error at app start.

Version 1.1.8 November 4, 2026

  • Installer fixes.

Version 1.1.7 November 4, 2026

  • The installer closes running copies of the app before installing.

Version 1.1.6 November 4, 2026

  • Clearer button text while an update downloads.

Version 1.1.5 November 4, 2026

  • The update installer starts only after the app has closed.

Version 1.1.4 November 4, 2026

  • Tooltip on the version number in the header.

Version 1.1.3 November 4, 2026

  • Versions are published in the public deadlock-assist-releases repository.

Version 1.1.2 November 4, 2026

  • Installed version number in the app header.

Version 1.1.1 November 4, 2026

  • Installer in English.

Version 1.1.0 November 4, 2026

  • Updates from GitHub: the app checks for a new version by itself and installs it in one click.

Version 1.0.0 November 4, 2026

  • First installer for Windows.
  • Custom app icon.
  • In-game overlay: what to buy now, counters to enemies under Tab, enemy purchases, clock and camps.
  • Item advisor based on statistics, other players’ builds and win rates against enemies.
  • Match history with both teams’ items and purchase order.
  • Game data: heroes, items and patch changes.

The app updates itself. You only download it once.
