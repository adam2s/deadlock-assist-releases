Changelog

What's new in every version. The app updates itself.

Version 1.2.9 November 5, 2026

  • “Website” button in the header opens the project website deadlock-assist.com (the English page when the app is in English).

Version 1.2.8 November 5, 2026

  • English interface: the PL / EN switch in the header changes the language of the window, the overlay, the advisor's reasons, “What now” hints and game names. On first start the language follows Windows.
  • The list of changes in “Updates” is in English too.

Version 1.2.7 November 5, 2026

  • Match history: every advisor suggestion shows why you got it: which card row it was on (“Your build” or “Vs enemies”) and its reasons (enemy threat, chance to win, popularity on your hero, build, team).
  • Below the suggestions table, an expandable note explains each kind of reason. Matches from before this version have no saved reasons.
Older versions (1.0.0–1.2.6)

Version 1.2.6 November 5, 2026

  • Item advisor: the “Win” label compares items with the buyers' farm evened out. Before, expensive items bought by players already ahead looked better than they are. On 2020 real matches from the last 2 weeks, 81% of suggestions are good against the enemy team instead of 62%.
  • The advisor no longer suggests items almost nobody on your hero buys at this stage of the match (no win data).
  • The advisor now matches players' real next purchase 29% of the time instead of 25%.
  • The advisor also judges heroes' healing, crowd control, durability and mobility from their ability descriptions (e.g. Dynamo's and Mo & Krill's stuns).
  • The “Buy now” card has two rows: “Your build” (the build's next item, how many souls are missing and roughly when you will have them) and “Vs enemies” (one item against the whole team's biggest threat, e.g. “enemy healing: Abrams, Ivy”).
  • Until 10:00 the “Vs enemies” row answers the two enemies in your lane (e.g. “in lane, enemy healing: Lash”), then the whole team. The separate “In lane” section is gone.
  • When one enemy has far more souls than the rest (read from under the portraits), the card shows them with an item against them.
  • Advisor settings: three modes (“My build”, “Balanced”, “Counters”) instead of sliders; the sliders are under “Advanced”, with simpler names.
  • Reasons on the card during the game are shorter and without percentage points; the numbers stay in the Tab view.
  • The advisor recognises your play style (weapon, spirit, durability) from the items you buy, even before it reads stats from the HUD.

Version 1.2.5 November 4, 2026

  • “Suggest a change” button next to the tabs: a short form opens a ready idea report on GitHub in your browser.
  • “☕ Support” button in the top right corner of the app window (not in the in-game overlay).

Version 1.2.4 November 4, 2026

  • “Overlay” tab: the screen preview is in the middle with settings on both sides; the preview stays in place while you scroll, so you set the last options while seeing the whole screen.
  • Clicking a panel in the preview scrolls to its settings.
  • Clock panel in the overlay: “What now” hints have an icon with a direction arrow and a second line with what to do; camps show which part of the map they are in and a bar until respawn; timers are split into “Camps and bosses” and “Events”.

Version 1.2.3 November 4, 2026

  • The farm chart no longer drops real readings from under the portraits when souls grow fast (e.g. early in the match); it only drops misreads.
  • Tests comparing top bar farm with farm counted from items on a recorded match.

Version 1.2.2 November 4, 2026

  • History: farm chart by match minute, for you, both team averages and every player, compared with a typical player.
  • Every half minute the assistant reads all players' souls from under the top bar portraits; older matches count farm from item prices from Tab.
  • History: table of farm gain every 5 minutes (souls per minute).
  • History: a summary at the end of the match with what to improve next time (farm, unspent souls, advisor hints, big enemy purchases).
  • The assistant now saves your unspent souls every half minute, for the match summary.

Version 1.2.1 November 4, 2026

  • Faster in the background: the game dictionary and match history are kept in memory instead of read from the database for every piece of advice.
  • Less data sent to the app and overlay windows.
  • Code cleanup with no change in behaviour.

Version 1.2.0 November 4, 2026

  • New “Updates” tab with the list of changes in every version.

Version 1.1.0 November 4, 2026

Version 1.0.0 November 4, 2026

  • First installer for Windows.
  • Custom app icon.
  • In-game overlay: what to buy now, counters to enemies under Tab, enemy purchases, clock and camps.
  • Item advisor based on statistics, other players' builds and win rates against enemies.

  • Game data: heroes, items and patch changes.
